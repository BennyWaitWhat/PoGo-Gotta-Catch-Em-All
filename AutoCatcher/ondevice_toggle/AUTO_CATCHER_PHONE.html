Pokémon GO Auto-Catcher — runs on your phone

No desktop. No Tailscale. No me. Tap a button, walk out the door.

✅What changed

The catcher used to live on the desktop: Python + the frida library ran on the PC and reached your phone's in-game gadget over the network. Nothing about it was magic — it was just those two pieces.

So I built those same two pieces directly inside your phone's Termux: I compiled frida 17.9.11 on-device (against Frida's official Android core devkit) and run the exact catcher there, pointed at the loopback gadget 127.0.0.1:27047. The desktop is now completely out of the loop.

Verified live: it connects, finds every singleton with zero faults, throws Excellent, re-throws on break-out, feeds berries, tracks server-banked stardust — and catches. All on the phone.

🎯How to use it

1
Open Pokémon GO and get to the map (so spawns are around you).
2
Swipe down the notification shade. You'll see the toggle:
⚪ Auto-Catcher: OFF
Open Pokémon GO, then tap TURN ON.
TURN ON
🎯 Auto-Catcher: ON
Catching nearby Pokémon. Tap TURN OFF to stop.
TURN OFF
3
Tap TURN ON. It starts catching. Tap TURN OFF when you're done.

The notification is persistent (it re-appears after a reboot), so the toggle is always one swipe away.

🧠How it works under the hood

[ Pokémon GO ]  in-process Frida gadget, LISTEN mode on 127.0.0.1:27047
       │  (loopback — never leaves the phone)
[ Termux ]  python3.13  +  frida 17.9.11 (compiled on-device)
       │  attaches to "Gadget", runs the catch script + liveness walk
[ Toggle ]  a notification button → catcher_ctl.sh start|stop
       │  start: launches the catcher under Termux's service
       └  stop : kills it cleanly (gadget frees)

The liveness GC-walk (which faulted when it ran inside the game's render thread) is safe here for the same reason it was safe on the desktop: it runs on a Frida RPC thread, off the render thread. The phone is just hosting the Frida client locally now.

🛟Troubleshooting

Button does nothing? Make sure Pokémon GO is actually open first. Or start it from a Termux session directly:

~/pogo/catcher_ctl.sh start     # turn on
~/pogo/catcher_ctl.sh stop      # turn off
~/pogo/catcher_ctl.sh status    # ON / OFF

Watch it work: tail -f ~/pogo/system/analysis/pokemod/catch_loop.log

Don't run pkg upgrade in Termux. The Termux package manager uses symlinks, and on this Android the app can't execute symlinks — that's the whole reason the build was hard. An upgrade re-creates them and would break python/frida/notifications. If it ever happens, re-run the repair scripts in ondevice_toggle/ (fix_bin.sh, fix_libexec.sh, build_applibs.sh).
It catches whatever spawns near your avatar, including things you might want to keep — your existing tag/keep rules and the cull tooling still apply separately. The catcher only catches; it never transfers.
