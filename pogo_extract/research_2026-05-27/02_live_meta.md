## 6. Spotlight Hour — Paused This Season

There are **NO weekly Tuesday Spotlight Hours** in May 2026. Niantic explicitly retired weekly Spotlight Hours starting the Memories in Motion season (Mar 3 2026); they have been replaced by **Showcase Tuesday** (10 a.m.–8 p.m. local, ~20 PokéStop Showcase categories).

Showcase Tuesdays confirmed running May 5 / 12 / 19 / 26, 2026 — featured species categories not consistently published in summary form ([Nintendo Wire weekly guides](https://nintendowire.com/guides/pokemon-go/showcase-tuesday-for-may-26th-2026/)).

Forever Forward (June 2 onward) reportedly expands Showcase Tuesday to allow **5 entries per Trainer** ([comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-go-forever-forward-season-details-pokemon-debuts-showcase-changes/)).

## 13. Niantic / Scopely Status

**Scopely's $3.5B acquisition of Niantic's gaming business (including Pokémon GO) CLOSED 2025-05-29**, ~3 months after the March 2025 announcement.

- Acquired: Pokémon GO, Pikmin Bloom, Monster Hunter Now, Campfire, Wayfarer + ~400 staff
- Niantic spun off **Niantic Spatial Inc.** (John Hanke; geospatial AI, retains Ingress Prime & Peridot)
- Total deal value ~$3.85B (incl. $350M cash)
- "Business as usual" for PoGo team per launch communications
- ([Variety](https://variety.com/2025/gaming/news/scopely-pokemon-go-niantic-sale-3-billion-1236334814/), [Hub](https://pokemongohub.net/post/news/scopely-completes-the-acquisition-of-niantics-game-business/), [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokemon-go-under-new-ownership-as-scopely-closes-35bn-niantic-deal/))

**Cadence impact**: Visible in 2025-2026 — Niantic/Scopely shifted from paid GO Fest ticket model toward **GO Pass progression** (free + paid tracks); Global GO Fest 2026 is free for the first time; "Memories in Motion" itself was tagged as a return to a more regular event schedule with weekend events consolidated to Saturdays.
